# Lead discovery and outreach playbook

- Discovery is free: OpenStreetMap Overpass for businesses, plus the business's own website for the contact address. Never scrape paid directories.
- Filter to the right city and category before storing. Unfiltered dumps produce unusable leads and waste the whole run.
- Score leads on: has website or not, page speed, mobile viewport, HTTPS, contact reachability, business size signals.
- Compliance gate: an unknown email is skipped, not guessed. No unsubscribe route, no send.
- Four step sales flow: (1) short value email, (2) requirement form, (3) free concept wireframe only after interest, (4) build and payment terms.
- Never build the full site before budget and scope are agreed in writing.
- Track every send and reply; auto follow-up only once, then stop.
