# Client delivery playbook

1. Intake: name, business, one-line goal, budget range, deadline, who approves. Missing budget means no quote.
2. Send a one page scope: pages or endpoints, what is included, what is not, revision count, timeline, price, payment split (50/50 or 40/40/20).
3. Build in a repo the client can be shown. Commit daily; do not wait for perfection to show progress.
4. Deliver with: live URL, repo, credentials handover, short video or doc on how to edit content, and 7 days bug support in writing.
5. Ask for the testimonial and the referral at handover, while satisfaction is highest.
